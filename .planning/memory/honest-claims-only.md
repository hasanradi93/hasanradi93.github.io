description: The site states only facts about the products; no invented customers, ratings or behaviour
---

The site may say only what the products really do. It shows no testimonials, customer counts or ratings. Product visuals are labelled as illustrative sketches.

The SQLNext sketch once showed a zero-date fix that SQLNext's docs do not describe. It was replaced with the documented DEFINER removal (error 1449).

**Why:** the domain will be reviewed by Paddle, and false claims are a risk for the business.

**How to apply:** before adding a claim, find it in the product's README or docs, or ask the owner.
